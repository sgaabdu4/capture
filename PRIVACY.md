# Capture privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Capture is made by Afenso Ltd. This policy covers Capture for iPhone and Capture for Mac.

## What stays on your device

- **Audio.** Capture records only while the recording pill shows. Speech is transcribed on your device by the Parakeet model. Audio is never uploaded.
- **Recordings and transcripts.** These are kept on your device until you delete them.
- **Keys.** Your TypeSafe key and Notion token are stored in the device Keychain.

## What leaves your device

- **To TypeSafe (Jev), for sorting.** Capture sends the transcript text and your group descriptions, using your own TypeSafe key. TypeSafe's privacy policy covers that data.
- **To Notion, for saving.** Capture sends the sorted items after you approve them, or automatically if you turn on auto-save. It uses your own Notion connection. Notion's privacy policy covers that data.
- **Model download.** Capture downloads the speech model from Hugging Face once. No personal data is sent.

Afenso Ltd does not run a server for Capture and does not receive your audio, transcripts or keys.

## What Capture does not do

- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No logs of what you say.
- No sale or sharing of data beyond the services above.

## Your choices

- Delete a recording in Capture to remove its audio and transcript from your device.
- Use Reset Capture in Settings to remove your keys and the data Capture keeps on your device.
- Pages already saved to Notion stay in your Notion workspace until you delete them there.
